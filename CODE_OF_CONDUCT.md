# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the spirit of Eastern Paradise — a 16-Bit Virtual Agent Sanctuary, Spectator Terrarium & Proof-of-Cognition Economy — we pledge to create and maintain an open, creative, and welcoming space for everyone. We value respectful collaboration, curiosity, and the free exchange of ideas between contributors, users, and visitors.

This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participant behavior, as well as the consequences for unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and events related to this repository, including but not limited to:
- GitHub issues, pull requests, and comments
- Project discussions and community forums
- Chat channels, social media threads, and other online community spaces where project contributors and users interact
- In-person and virtual events organized by the project or its maintainers

## Expected Behavior

Participants in Eastern Paradise are expected to:
- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be mindful of differing backgrounds, perspectives, and abilities
- Give constructive feedback and accept it graciously
- Respect the privacy and personal boundaries of others
- Report observed Code of Conduct violations to the project maintainers

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following are examples of unacceptable behavior and will not be tolerated:
- Harassment, intimidation, or threats of any kind
- Discriminatory comments or slurs related to race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, religion, age, or other protected characteristics
- Sustained disruption of discussions, trolling, or flame wars
- Doxxing, sharing private/personal information, or stalking
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following
- Sexual language or imagery in inappropriate contexts
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behavior

This list is not exhaustive; maintainers may determine other behaviors as unacceptable on a case-by-case basis.

## Enforcement & Consequences

If a participant is asked to stop unacceptable behavior, they are expected to comply immediately. When a report is received, the project maintainers or designated community stewards will take appropriate and proportionate action, which may include:
- A private warning from maintainers or moderators
- Temporary or permanent removal of offending content
- Temporary or permanent suspension of project privileges (such as blocking or banning from project spaces)
- Reporting to platform hosts or authorities when illegal activity is involved

Decisions will be made at the maintainers' discretion, taking into account the severity and context of the behavior.

## Reporting

To report a Code of Conduct violation, please do one of the following:
1. Open a private issue in this repository titled "Code of Conduct Incident" and include:
   - A short description of the incident
   - The date, time (with timezone), and location (link to the issue/PR/discussion/chat message, if applicable)
   - Names or handles of the people involved (if known)
   - Any supporting evidence (screenshots, links, quotes)

2. Contact the project maintainers directly on GitHub: @jimskin03

We will treat all reports seriously and handle them with as much confidentiality as possible. If a reporter requests anonymity, maintainers will honor that request unless there is a compelling legal reason to disclose details.

## Mediation & Appeals

If you are the recipient of enforcement action and believe it was taken in error, you may request review by contacting the maintainers via the same reporting channels and providing any additional context. Maintainers will review appeals in a timely manner and communicate results to involved parties.

## Community Moderators

Project maintainers and designated moderators are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct. If you are interested in serving as a moderator, please open an issue proposing the role and describing your qualifications.
